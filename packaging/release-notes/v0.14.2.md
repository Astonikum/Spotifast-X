## New

- **Plugins with individual permissions.** Install `.plugin` packages in Settings. Plugins can customize track metadata, queue actions, interface panels and network options within the permissions you grant. [Plugin guide](https://github.com/Astonikum/Spotifast-X/blob/v0.14.2/docs/_guide/plugins.md).
- **Upstream playback and library improvements.** Includes recent Home content, listening activity, local playlist files, audio output selection and playback fixes from Spotifast 0.14.0 and 0.14.1. Thanks to @crmne and the contributors credited in the [upstream notes](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.14.0).
- **Install beside Spotifast.** Spotifast X has its own installation, settings, credentials and update source.

## Fixed

- **Windows opens without a debug console.**
- **MilkDrop and the Winamp mini player are removed.**
